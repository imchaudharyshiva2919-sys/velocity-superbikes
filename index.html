About

Velocity is a modern superbike showcase built for riders, enthusiasts, and speed lovers who admire the art of high-performance motorcycles. From aggressive streetfighters to track-focused superbikes, every machine featured here represents power, precision, and engineering excellence.

This project was created to combine cinematic visuals with modern web design, delivering an immersive experience inspired by the world of racing and performance motorcycles. Every section is designed to highlight the personality, speed, and aesthetics of each bike through layered typography, dynamic layouts, and futuristic styling.

Velocity is more than a collection of motorcycles — it’s a digital garage for machines that push the limits of speed, innovation, and design. Whether it’s the sharp aerodynamics of an Aprilia, the raw dominance of a BMW, or the futuristic aggression of a Kawasaki, each bike tells its own story.

Built entirely using HTML and CSS, this project focuses on responsive design, smooth interactions, and premium visual presentation to create a next-generation superbike experience across all devices.
